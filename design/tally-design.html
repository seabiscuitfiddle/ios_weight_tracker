1Tally — a Modernist calorie & exercise tracker

One net number everywhere: net calories = food − exercise, shown as x / y against a goal auto-derived from your weight target. Flat, architectural, red-on-white, Archivo throughout. Log surfaces (lock, home, in-app) all lead with the same three metrics — net calories, protein, fiber — so the number you glance at on the lock screen is the number you edit in the app. A shared bottom tab bar — Today · Log · History · Progress — ties the in-app screens together; the accented Log tab opens the AI quick-log.
